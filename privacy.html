FaceGlow AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026. Applies to the FaceGlow AI Android app (com.faceglowai.app).

FaceGlow AI turns a face photo into a short personal skin and grooming plan, reminds you to follow it, and asks for a new photo at the end of each cycle to show what changed. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

Who we are

The app is operated by i techsearch Media. Contact for privacy questions, requests and complaints: deveshwarkota@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: the email address and name on the Google account you sign in with, and an account ID.
  • Your photos: the face photos you take in the app or choose from your library.
  • Measurements from the photo: simple quality numbers worked out on your phone (for example lighting, sharpness and head angle) so before and after photos can be compared fairly.
  • Results: descriptions of skin and grooming (for example hydration, oil balance, texture) shown as bands such as "Needs focus", "Developing" and "Strong", and descriptive face-structure tags. We do not produce an attractiveness score and we do not compare you with other people.
  • Your choices and activity: the goal you pick, optional answers about your skin or budget, your plan, and which daily tasks you tick off.
  • Consent records: when you accepted this policy and the AI provider that was named at that time.
  • Subscription status (once subscriptions are available): whether you have an active subscription, handled through Google Play.

Reminder times are stored on your phone only. We do not use advertising identifiers and we show no ads.

Why we use it

To create your report and 5-day plan, to compare your photos at the end of a cycle, to send the reminders you ask for, to keep your account secure, and to provide your subscription. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Sensitive information: your face photo and skin descriptions

A face photo, the numbers we work out from it, and descriptions of your skin can be treated as sensitive personal data, including biometric or health-related data, in some places (for example the EU and UK, and some US states such as Illinois, Texas and Washington). So we ask for your separate, explicit consent before your first scan, and we explain here exactly what happens:

  • What we do: your phone checks that the photo is usable (face present, angle, eyes open, lighting, sharpness). The photo is then analysed to describe skin and grooming. This involves measurements such as head angle and face size in the frame.
  • What we do not do: we do not use face recognition to identify you or anyone else, we do not match your face against other people or databases, we do not infer your race, ethnicity, age or gender, and we do not sell or share this data for advertising.
  • Consent and withdrawal: using the scan requires your explicit consent. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your photos or your account in the app, or by writing to us. Withdrawing means we cannot provide the scan and plan.
  • Destruction schedule: face photos are permanently deleted 1 month after they are taken, or immediately when you delete them or your account, whichever is first. The measurements and descriptions kept with your results are deleted when you delete your account.

Who processes it

  • Google (Gemini API, model: Google Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite): your photo is sent to this AI model to describe skin and grooming. We ask it for descriptions only, never to rate how you look. Google processes the photo under its own terms for the Gemini API.
  • Supabase: our database, file storage and sign-in provider. Photos are kept in a private storage folder that only your account can open.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat: to process and verify subscriptions, once subscriptions are available.

These providers act on our behalf. We do not share your photos with anyone else. We do not sell personal information.

Where your data goes

Our database and photo storage are hosted by Supabase in Japan. Google may process photos for the AI step in other countries. If you use the app outside India, your data is therefore transferred internationally. We rely on your consent and on our providers' contractual commitments for these transfers.

How long we keep it

  • Photos: kept for 1 month, then deleted automatically. You can delete them sooner at any time.
  • Results, plans and history: kept until you delete your account, so you can see your progress without the photos.
  • Account deletion: removes your account, photos, results, plans and history. A minimal record that a deletion happened is kept without your identity.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct, download or delete your data, to withdraw your consent, or to object to how we use it. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights, for example under the EU and UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, or US state privacy laws. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

  • Delete photos: in the app, Settings, Delete my photos.
  • Delete account and all data: in the app, Settings, Delete my account. If you cannot open the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Get a copy of your data: in the app, Settings, Download my data.
  • Withdraw consent, correct your data or complain: write to deveshwarkota@gmail.com. This is also our grievance contact under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We reply within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Photos are stored privately and are only shown to you through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a device lock.

Legal basis (EU and UK)

We process your account and plan data to provide the service you asked for. We process your face photo and skin descriptions only on the basis of your explicit consent.

Washington consumer health data

This policy also serves as our consumer health data privacy policy. The consumer health data we collect is the face photo and skin descriptions described above. We collect it only with your consent, use it only to provide your report and plan, share it only with the providers listed above, and do not sell it. You can withdraw consent, request access, and request deletion as described in this policy.

Age limit

FaceGlow AI is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

Not medical advice

FaceGlow AI describes skin and grooming. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. See a doctor or dermatologist for acne, a rash, pain or sudden changes.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app and ask for your consent again where the law requires it. The date at the top shows the latest version.
