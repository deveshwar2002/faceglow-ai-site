Delete your FaceGlow AI account

App: FaceGlow AI (com.faceglowai.app), operated by i techsearch Media.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open FaceGlow AI and go to Settings.
  2. Under Privacy and data, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

If you cannot open the app

Email deveshwarkota@gmail.com from the Google account email you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my FaceGlow AI account". We will confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • All your photos.
  • Your reports, plans, task history and progress.
  • Your consent records.

A minimal record that a deletion took place is kept without your name, email or any other identifying detail. Deletion cannot be undone.

Delete only your photos

To keep your account but remove the photos, open Settings and tap Delete my photos. Photos are also deleted automatically after 1 month.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
